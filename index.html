Our Story

Born to Dominate
the Land of Dawn

Demon Fox ESport was founded in 2021 by a group of elite MLBB players who shared one vision — to become the most feared team in competitive Mobile Legends. Starting from grassroots tournaments, we clawed our way to the top through relentless practice, strategic innovation, and an unbreakable team spirit.

Today, we stand as one of the top contenders in the MPL circuit, known for our aggressive playstyle, clutch team fights, and the never-surrender mentality that defines the Demon Fox way.
